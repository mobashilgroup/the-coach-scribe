Frequently asked questions

Is my clients' data private?

Yes. Access is isolated per coach and organization, audio is deleted after processing by default, and transcript retention is configurable. Nothing is shared without your explicit approval.

Does the AI make decisions for me?

No. The AI drafts summaries and suggested replies; you review, edit, and approve. Nothing reaches a client automatically.

Do I need consent to record?

The app captures consent before recording and helps you document it. You remain responsible for local laws, some of which require all-party consent.

What languages are supported?

English plus Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French, Hebrew and more, with right-to-left support.

About us

The Coach Scribe is an AI-powered assistant platform built for coaches and consultants. It was born from the real needs and guidance of dozens of coaches who wanted to focus on transformation rather than administration. The platform records, transcribes, summarizes and organizes coaching sessions, turning conversations into clear follow-up actions. The Coach Scribe is a product of Mobashil Group S.A., headquartered in Panama City, Panama.
